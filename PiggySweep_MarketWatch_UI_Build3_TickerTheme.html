📊 Understand your dashboards

Know what each screen means

Plan shows your monthly income allocation and goals. Dashboard summarises money and spending. Daily shows today's allowance and recent transactions. Budgets tracks category limits. Invest tracks portfolio values.

All your investments in one place

Track FD, mutual funds, PPF, NPS, stocks and gold. You enter the values, so no bank login is needed.
